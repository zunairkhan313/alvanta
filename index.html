Common questions

How long does a website take?

Most business websites take 2 to 4 weeks. Online stores and custom web apps take longer, and we confirm the timeline in your quote.

Can you work with my existing brand?

Yes. We can follow your current logo and colours, or refresh them if you want a new look.

Do you offer monthly marketing plans?

Yes. Social media, video, SEO and ads can all be handled on a monthly plan.

How do payments work?

Projects are split into milestones. Monthly plans are billed at the start of each month.

Will I own the final files?

Yes. Once your project is paid in full, you get the source files and full ownership.
